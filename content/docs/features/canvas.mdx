<Callout title="Comparison scope">
  Univer is measured as the complete Office SDK together with optional modules. Miro is measured on paid cloud plans, and the available capabilities depend on the plan. Excalidraw refers to the free open-source application and Excalidraw.com, excluding Plus.

  Collaboration, history, and format conversion require the corresponding modules and services. Native embedding between Univer products counts toward the capability, and the required modules are listed. Editing or calculation performed by an external application is not a native canvas capability. This comparison covers only the product's own non-AI capabilities, and separate external products and add-ons are excluded.
</Callout>

“Open source” indicates that the open-source edition supports the feature in full. A row without that mark may cover only a basic subset, may require an optional module, or may require a commercial license. The corresponding module documentation provides the details.
