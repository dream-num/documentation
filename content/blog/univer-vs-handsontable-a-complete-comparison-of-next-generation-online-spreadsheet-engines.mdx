## What are you building?

Handsontable and Univer both let developers embed editable tables in web applications. The useful distinction is the product experience you need: an editable data grid, or an Office-style workbook that can become part of a broader editing and collaboration workflow.

[Handsontable](https://handsontable.com/docs/react-data-grid/) provides a spreadsheet-like data grid with cell editing, validation, sorting, filtering and framework integrations. [Univer](/overview) provides embedded Office editors, including Sheets, with workbook editing, formulas and programmable APIs.

## Compare the requirements

| Requirement | Univer | Handsontable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spreadsheet editing | Workbook and worksheet editing, formatting and formulas | Spreadsheet-like data grid with editing and data-management features |
| Application integration | Presets, plugins and Facade APIs | Configuration, hooks, plugins and framework wrappers |
| Formulas | Univer formula engine; open-source and commercial capabilities differ | Formula integration through HyperFormula; review its configuration and license separately |
| Office workflows | Sheets can be combined with Docs, Slides and other Univer editors | Evaluate the grid alongside any separate document or presentation tools your product needs |
| File compatibility | Office import/export is a commercial capability; test representative files | Check the current import/export features against the file formats you need |
| Multi-user workflows | Commercial collaboration services, integrated with your own accounts and permissions | Verify the collaboration integration required by your application; this article does not benchmark a shared-editing deployment |

Feature availability depends on the package, edition and version. This is a requirements checklist, not a claim that every feature is included in the free download.

## Keep licensing separate from features

Univer's [open-source repository](https://github.com/dream-num/univer) uses Apache-2.0. The open-source distribution contains frontend Sheets and Docs editor capabilities, not the entire commercial Office suite or its server services. Collaboration, Office import/export, charts, pivot tables and other Pro capabilities have separate commercial terms. Review the [edition comparison and pricing](https://univer.ai/pricing/).

Handsontable offers [commercial and non-commercial/evaluation licensing](https://handsontable.com/docs/react-data-grid/license-key/). A non-commercial license is not a free commercial Community edition. Check the terms for your intended use, including any formula engine dependency.

## Evaluate with your own workload

Use the same files and operations for both evaluations: initial load, scrolling, paste, recalculation and save/export. Record the browser, device, dataset and enabled features. Large row counts alone do not establish a useful performance comparison, and this article makes no universal speed or concurrency claim.

Choose an editable grid when the main task is managing application records. Evaluate Univer when users need Office-style content that remains editable inside your product, with optional collaboration and file processing. Start with the [Sheets overview](/guides/sheets) and test the workflows you intend to ship.
